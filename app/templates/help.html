Citizen Assistance & Documentation

Learn how to register, file complaints, attach valid evidence, and track investigation progress.

Demonstration & Portfolio Platform: CivicShield AI is an educational technology system showcasing full-stack Python engineering, relational database management, and machine learning integration. In an immediate police emergency, dial 112 or visit your local police station.
1. How to File an Incident Complaint

CivicShield AI breaks down complaint filing into a guided 4-step wizard:

Step 1 — Incident Narrative

Provide an accurate title, incident date/time, and clear narrative. Our machine learning system will discreetly analyze the text and suggest the most applicable crime category.

Step 2 — Location & Station

Select your district, specify the landmark or exact street address, and choose the jurisdiction police station responsible for the area.

Step 3 — Evidence Documents

Attach supporting evidence such as digital photos of damage, transaction invoices, or PDF records. Preview files or remove unwanted ones prior to final submission.

Step 4 — Review & Reference

Review all entered details, confirm accuracy, and submit to generate your unique tracking reference number (e.g. CS-2026-XXXXXX).

2. Evidence Requirements & Security

Quality evidence expedites investigation. Follow these standards:

  • Permitted File Formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, and PDF.
  • Size Limitations: Up to 16 MB per file. Multiple files can be attached to a single complaint.
  • Protected Server Storage: Uploaded evidence is stored in private, protected directories—never in public web static folders.
  • Access Control: Evidence can only be accessed and downloaded by the complainant who uploaded it, the assigned investigating officer, or authorized administrators. Every download is logged in the audit trail.
3. What Does Each Status Mean?
Status Badge Operational Meaning
Pending Complaint has been registered and is awaiting initial station review and verification.
Under Investigation Complaint is verified and assigned to an investigating officer actively handling the case.
Evidence Collected Key witness statements, forensic or document proofs, and digital records have been gathered.
Action Required The investigating officer requires additional information or document copies from the complainant.
Resolved Investigation concluded, report filed, and appropriate administrative or legal action taken.
Rejected Complaint evaluated and closed (e.g. jurisdiction mismatch, non-criminal dispute, or duplicate submission) with official remarks provided.
